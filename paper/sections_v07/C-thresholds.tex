\section{Preregistered criteria and recorded verdicts}
\label{app:thresholds}

The criteria below are taken from \filepath{docs/v07/PREREGISTRATION.md}, committed before any
holdout bank had been played and before the sealed adversary half had been decoded. The verdict
labels and thresholds are the protocol's own; the rule wording in the tables is condensed from the
protocol, and the source document carries the exact text. Three things are kept distinct: the
\emph{registered rule}, which is a quotation or close paraphrase of the protocol; the
\emph{recorded verdict}, which applies that rule as written and is the verdict of the phase-5
record; and the \emph{reading with uncertainty}, which is later commentary added in this revision
and not part of the registration. The verdicts are reported in \S\ref{sec:results} and
\S\ref{sec:results-nonconfirm}.

\subsection*{Primary criterion}

\sestina{} beats \fcheap{} on both primary holdout banks, pooled over \vsevenBtwoFcheapN{} games.

\begin{center}\tabfont
\begin{tabularx}{\linewidth}{@{}l L@{}}
\toprule
Verdict & Condition \\
\midrule
CERTIFIED advancement & pooled lower bound above \vsevenPrimaryFloor{}, sign replicates on both banks, gate passes \\
measured advancement & pooled lower bound above 0 but under \vsevenPrimaryFloor{}, sign replicates, gate passes \\
not an advancement & the pooled interval contains zero, or the sign does not replicate, or the gate fails \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabularx}
\end{center}

\noindent
The verdict labels above are the protocol's own. This paper does not use the word \emph{certified},
which suggests verification by a third party, and reports the top outcome instead as what was
actually satisfied (\S\ref{sec:design-protocol}). Recorded verdict: the comparison
\vsevenPrimaryVerdict{}. Reading with uncertainty: the rule is stated on the pooled lower bound and
is resolved.

The protocol's ``eight changed-partner rows'' counts the self-play row among them; \S\ref{sec:results-partners}
reports \vsevenSonePositive{} of the \vsevenSoneRows{} changed rows, excluding self-play. Both row counts give
the same recorded verdict, and the phase-5 recording carries the ambiguity as deviation D9. The
\textsc{pass} entries for S3, S4, S6 and S7 are the phase-5 record's verdicts and are not computed
by this paper's generator; S1, S2 and S5 are recomputed from the artifacts.

A failure of S1, S2 or S6 would have been a substantive failure, and the paper would report
\sestina{} as brittle in that named way. A failure of S7 would have been procedural and the
evaluation would have stopped.

\subsection*{Non-confirmation criteria}

The seven conditions are answered in \S\ref{sec:results-seven}, with the reading each uses. Two are
met or unresolved. The protocol's own closing provision is quoted in full, because this paper relies
on it:

\begin{quote}\small
``And the honest all-negative path is a legitimate deliverable. If phase~5 returns `no advancement over
the frontier', the v0.7 report is a barrier report: the four ledger entries phase~3 closed with
measurements, the two corrections to the corpus's own record, the mechanical side-channel gate with its
three calibrated positive controls, and the S6 residual this document commits. That is a real
contribution and the report should not apologise for it.''
\end{quote}
