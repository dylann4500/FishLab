\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

\sestina{} combines constraint-based inference, a linear action policy, public-history
tie-breaking, half-suit contestation, stall detection, and guarded search for six-player Canadian
Fish. Against the preregistered \fcheap{} baseline, the recorded implementation produced an
observed win-rate edge of \vsevenBtwoFcheap{}~pp relative to parity over \vsevenBtwoFcheapN{}
holdout games; the reported deal-bootstrap interval, $[\vsevenBtwoFcheapLo, \vsevenBtwoFcheapHi]$,
is nominal, because persistent cross-deal agent state violates its independence assumption, and the
registered decision rule was satisfied on that nominal interval. Its comparison with the phase-2
composite is unresolved, at \vsevenBtwoComposite{}~pp
$[\vsevenBtwoCompositeLo, \vsevenBtwoCompositeHi]$, and the component-attribution criterion is not
consistently satisfied across banks.

The evaluation includes partner substitution, cross-play, rule variants, and adversarial-response
searches. These tests provide evidence within the evaluated configurations and budgets, with
unresolved partner-threshold uncertainty and known implementation limitations: the partner row that
decides the registered coordination criterion is \vsevenSoneMin{}~pp
$[\vsevenOppFiveWithholderLo, \vsevenOppFiveWithholderHi]$, above the $\vsevenSoneThreshold{}$
threshold on the point estimate but not resolved against it (\S\ref{sec:results-partners}); over
the shared panel \sestina{} is \vsevenRegretFrozenRank{}rd of four on empirical minimax regret with
a worst cell that does not replicate in sign (\S\ref{sec:results-panel}); and every scored cell ran
in an execution mode that retains a known cross-deal state residual in searching agents
(\S\ref{sec:discussion-engineering}). None of the \vsevenBfourArmsWord{} tested responses, including
a transcript-inversion adversary using a \vsix{} surrogate of the target, achieves a positive edge,
but the study does not bound the performance of stronger or differently constructed adversaries.
Four candidate mechanisms did not demonstrate an additional improvement under the tested
conditions, and each result is reported with the measurement and the limitation that scope it
(\S\ref{sec:development-rejected}).

The main outcomes are an observed improvement over the specified baseline under the recorded
implementation, with nominal rather than coverage-validated uncertainty, an exact reference
computation for the constraint-conditioned belief model, and an evaluation procedure that separates
observed gains from unresolved differences. The transferable design elements are the constraint
factorisation and count program, the duplicate-deal design with held-out banks and a sign-agreement
criterion, and the calibration of adversarial searches against planted handicaps. The first priority for
further work is a corrected scored rerun with agent state reset between deals, which is required
before any interval in this paper can be read as coverage-validated; after it come stronger
adversarial searches, testing whether small improvements compound, and comparison with
independently developed agents (\S\ref{sec:discussion-future}).
